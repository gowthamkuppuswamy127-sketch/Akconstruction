01 — Home Renovation

Renovating a home you are still living in

Full-home and room-by-room remodels, sequenced so the disruption is contained and you know exactly which week each part of the house is out of use.

We start by measuring the existing structure rather than working from the builder's original plan, because after twenty years they rarely match. From there you get a drawing, an itemised estimate, and a week-by-week sequence.

Typically includes

  • Layout changes and non-structural demolition
  • Flooring — tile, vitrified, wooden
  • Plumbing and electrical rework
  • Kitchen and bathroom renovation
  • Putty, primer and finish painting
  • Doors, windows and hardware

02 — Interior Fit-Out

Joinery that is still working in five years

Modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings, panelling and painting — built to the drawing, with hardware you can actually get serviced later.

We name the board, the laminate and the hinge brand in the estimate. Cheap hardware is the single most common reason a good-looking kitchen stops closing properly within two years, so we do not quote it.

Typically includes

  • Modular kitchens with named hardware
  • Wardrobes, lofts and storage
  • Gypsum and POP false ceilings
  • TV units, wall panelling, partitions
  • Lighting layout and fixtures
  • Final painting and finishing

03 — New Construction

Independent houses, foundation to handover

Residential builds run as a single accountable contract, including drawings, approvals coordination, structure, finishes and handover.

You deal with one firm and one engineer for the whole build. Stage payments are tied to completed, inspected work — not to a calendar.

Typically includes

  • Architectural and structural drawings
  • Approvals and sanction coordination
  • Foundation, RCC frame and slabs
  • Blockwork, plastering, waterproofing
  • Plumbing, electrical and finishes
  • Snagging and formal handover

04 — Structural & Civil

Work that has to be right the first time

RCC work, additional floors, extensions and structural repair — carried out to engineer-approved drawings, never by rule of thumb.

Adding a floor or removing a wall changes how load travels through the whole building. We will not quote structural work without a structural drawing, and we will tell you plainly if what you are asking for is not advisable.

Typically includes

  • Additional floors and terrace rooms
  • Extensions and setback construction
  • Column, beam and slab work
  • Structural crack assessment and repair
  • Column jacketing and strengthening
  • Load-bearing wall modifications

05 — Waterproofing & Repairs

Finding the leak, not hiding the stain

Terrace, bathroom and external wall treatment that addresses the source of the seepage rather than painting over the symptom.

Most repeat leaks are repeat leaks because the first contractor treated the wall where the damp appeared instead of the junction where the water entered. We trace it first, then quote.

Typically includes

  • Terrace and roof waterproofing
  • Bathroom and wet-area treatment
  • External wall and facade sealing
  • Sunken slab and drainage correction
  • Damp and seepage investigation
  • Post-repair finishing and painting

06 — Turnkey Management

One number to call about the whole project

Drawings, materials, labour, inspections and handover coordinated under one contract, so you are not chasing five vendors who each blame the others.

This is the option most of our customers choose. You approve the drawing and the estimate; we run everything else and report weekly.

Typically includes

  • Single point of contact throughout
  • Design and drawing coordination
  • Material procurement and verification
  • Vendor and labour management
  • Weekly written progress reports
  • Snag list closure and handover

07 — Real Estate & Property

Property handled with the same care as the buildings we construct

Alongside construction, Anil Kumar works directly as a real estate agent — specialising in residential and commercial rentals, land sales and agricultural properties, with expert service in leasing, buying and selling.

Because the same firm can also construct, renovate or repair what you are buying, you get a straight opinion on the property itself, not just help closing the deal.

Typically includes

  • Residential & commercial rentals
  • Land and agricultural property sales
  • Independent houses & residential plots
  • Hotel and warehouse properties
  • Leasing, buying and selling
  • Government-approved dealings

How it works

Step 03

Execution on site

Step 04

Handover & support

Snag list closed, bills handed over, and we stay reachable afterwards.
