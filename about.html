01 — Our story

Anil Kumar has been building and dealing property in Bengaluru since 2014.

Ak Construction and Interiors is a proprietorship run directly by Anil Kumar — not a call centre in front of a rotating cast of contractors. Since 2014 the firm has done two things side by side: building construction of every kind, and real estate — helping people buy, sell and lease residential, commercial and agricultural property.

We still do all types of construction-related work and interior works ourselves, and we still deal in property ourselves. One firm, one proprietor, both parts of the business answerable to the same phone number.

The whole proposition comes down to one line Anil says often: mainly, we are giving the best service for customers — and it is why most new work now comes from people we have already built for, or already helped buy or sell a property.

03 — How we work

Built on three commitments

These are not slogans. Each one corresponds to something we actually do differently on site.

01

Transparency

Itemised estimates against a measured drawing, named material brands, and shared bills. Any change in scope is priced and approved in writing before the work happens.

02

Safety on site

Scaffolding, edge protection and protective equipment are non-negotiable, on a two-room renovation exactly as much as on a full build.

03

Engineering precision

Structural work follows engineer-approved drawings. Nothing load-bearing is decided by eye on the day, and every stage is checked before the next one covers it.
